DEV STATE — DRIVES THE GAUGES, NOT A GAME MECHANIC

These four write to state. The instruments only ever read it — there is no second copy of the number. Nothing recharges: no recharge rate has been designed yet and inventing one here would be a lie.
PAD SIDE lit = flight pad on the RIGHT, right thumb flies, throttle on the left edge. Unlit = pad on the left. Nothing to do with handedness.

RADIAL lit = curve applied to the stick vector, total rate capped at the maximum. Unlit = per-axis curve as written in §4.1, where a 45° corner drag gives full pitch AND full roll at once — about 1.41× the stated max. To feel the difference: drag hard into the corner of the pad and hold. Straight pitch and straight roll are identical either way.
